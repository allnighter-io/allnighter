# Allnighter — Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective August 7, 2026**

This page states plainly how billing, cancellation, refunds, and the free trial
work. It restates parts of the [Terms of Service](Terms_of_Service.md) §2–3 in
one place; where the two differ, the Terms of Service governs.

Allnighter is downloadable software sold by **Happy Moose Apps Inc.** There is
nothing to ship, so there is no shipping or delivery policy. Access is granted
immediately on purchase.

---

## What You Are Buying

A subscription to Allnighter's orchestration features. Prices are in **US
dollars (USD)** and exclude any taxes you may owe; Canadian customers may be
charged GST/HST where applicable. Current prices are on
[allnighter.io](https://allnighter.io/#pricing).

## Refunds

**If the software does not work for you, email support@allnighter.io within 30
days of your purchase and we will refund you in full.** You do not need to
justify the request. We would rather refund you than argue.

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method, normally within 5–10
  business days once approved.
- For an annual subscription refunded after the 30-day window, we will refund
  the unused whole months at our discretion.
- If we terminate your entitlement for breach of the Terms, we refund the unused
  portion.
- Your statutory refund rights, including those under British Columbia consumer
  protection law, are unaffected by anything on this page.

What is **not** refundable: charges from your AI providers. Allnighter does not
resell model access, so your subscriptions with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or
anyone else are billed by them under their own terms and are outside our
control. If a provider suspends, limits, reprices, or terminates your access,
that is between you and them — see [Terms of Service](Terms_of_Service.md) §5.

## Cancellation

**Cancel any time.** There is no cancellation fee, no notice period, and no
retention maze — one action in your account.

- You keep paid access through the end of the period you have already paid for.
- At the end of that period you return to the **Free** tier. The software does
  not stop working, and no feature is taken away — only the number of runs per
  day is limited again.
- You keep your run history, and can export it at any time.
- We do not automatically refund the remainder of a period on cancellation, but
  see the 30-day promise above.

## Automatic Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel — monthly plans monthly,
annual plans annually, at the price you are currently paying. **Price changes
apply to new customers only:** while your subscription stays active, your price
does not change. We tell you in advance of any change, including when it does
not affect you.

The **Founding Builder** purchase is a one-time payment, not a subscription. It
does not renew and it is not revocable by us.

## Free Trial

- **14 days of unlimited runs, free.** No card is required to start it.
- The trial begins at **your first run** — not at install — so time does not
  burn while you are setting up.
- One trial per computer. Reinstalling, deleting local data, creating a new
  account, or changing your system clock does not grant a new one; the trial is
  recorded on our servers.
- **Nothing happens automatically when the trial ends.** It does not convert
  into a paid subscription and you are not charged. You simply return to the
  Free tier.

## Getting Help

Email **support@allnighter.io**. A real person reads it. If something is
broken, tell us before you dispute a charge — we will almost certainly just
refund you.
