Colour

Three colours only — neutral ink, amber, red — per product law. Round 1's green status dots and green/yellow capacity fills are gone: "fine" states are quiet neutral, amber marks only needs-attention (out-of-date host, the subscription about to hit its limit), red is reserved for failure and appears nowhere on this pane today.

Alternates considered

"Every AI subscription you pay for, from inside the one you're already in." — Round 1's headline. Emotionally empty: it says nothing concrete in the first six words and starts a long sentence. The viral post front-loads the shock — X inside Y, two products the reader already pays for. Replaced by the dynamic template above.

"The biggest cheat-code in your terminal." — Borrows the viral post's hype instead of its structure. In a settings pane it reads as marketing, and the voice is breathless where the brand is instrument-panel. Rejected.

"Stop copy-pasting between your CLIs." — Leads with a chore removed, not a gain claimed. It undersells parallel teams entirely and says nothing about capacity, which is the one argument no hand-rolled pairing can match. Rejected.

Eyebrow "IF YOU DON'T KNOW". — Worked inside the tweet it came from; standing alone above a settings pane it is confusing and faintly condescending. Dropped in round 2, no replacement.
